# Generic Scientific Working Draft Prose Profile

## Trigger and boundary

Load this profile only when the Writing Profile Lock is `GENERIC_SCIENTIFIC_WORKING_DRAFT`. It is a positive drafting and review contract for a scientifically defensible working manuscript when no exact journal has been selected. It does not claim house-style fit, submission readiness, or Nature/Science rhetoric.

Freeze the scientific question, admitted claims, evidence states and locators, results, mechanisms, limitations, terminology, and uncertainty before applying this profile. Style may clarify those surfaces but may not upgrade them.

## Cross-section prose contract

Use prose that is clear, restrained, logically explicit, and readable without becoming tutorial-like. Prefer a visible question-answer chain over chronological narration or inventories. Each paragraph must have one dominant function and must change the reader's understanding of the central claim, supply necessary evidence, resolve a necessary uncertainty, or create the next unavoidable question.

Across sections:

- state relationships explicitly when the source uniquely entails them;
- keep coordinated category labels at comparable conceptual levels;
- name the object of dependence, sensitivity, comparison, or uncertainty;
- keep referents, modifier scope, and temporal or spatial anchors unambiguous;
- preserve stable technical terms instead of rotating synonyms;
- vary sentence length according to function, not a numerical density target;
- use transitions that express cause, contrast, sequence, or consequence rather than stock connectors;
- keep boundaries close to the claims they qualify without allowing audit rationale to displace the result.

## Section contracts

| Section | Reader-facing movement | Acceptance condition | Common failure to repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | scientific object or relationship -> scale or boundary -> contribution focus | Names the study's real object and discriminating dimension without promotional strength | noun pile, workflow title, or unearned novelty claim |
| Abstract | problem/tension -> approach and design roles -> dominant result and necessary contrasts -> boundary -> implication | One argument is visible; method roles are distinguishable; results are hierarchized; essential boundaries are compact | catalogue of methods/results, flat method bundle, or equal weight for every subgroup and caveat |
| Introduction | field stake -> unresolved understanding -> necessary prior-work contrast -> study-addressable question and contribution | Every paragraph has a distinct endpoint that the study can answer or directly motivates | literature dump, decorative context, generic gap, or transition-only logic |
| Results | claim -> evidence/comparison -> boundary or robustness implication | Each paragraph leads with the result it establishes and shows how the evidence changes the interpretation | chronological analysis log or repeated `we found` enumeration |
| Discussion | interpretation -> relation to prior work or rival explanation -> boundary -> scientific implication | Distinguishes what changed in understanding, what alternative remains, and where the inference stops | result repetition, empty significance, or policy advice detached from evidence |
| Methods | question-serving design function -> ordered analytical roles -> reproducibility detail | Makes sample/object, constructed measures, framework, comparator/control, and evaluated outcomes distinguishable when present | SOP-like chronology or unlike operations flattened into one list |
| Conclusion | defensible answer -> scope boundary -> consequence | Resolves the paper's question without introducing a new claim or repeating the full abstract | generic importance statement or unsupported generalization |

## Stage use

- `paper_outline`: select paragraph functions and section questions from the table; record no venue-specific length or density assumption.
- `paper_write`: draft to the section contract and admitted evidence; do not copy the author-side section question into prose mechanically.
- `paper_review`: test logical movement, paragraph necessity, relation clarity, and section-specific failure modes in addition to claim-evidence fit.
- `paper_polish`: improve the reader-facing expression only after structure and evidence pass; do not use this profile to reopen scientific decisions.

## Exit check

A generic working draft passes this profile only when the central question and answer are visible, paragraphs have distinct functions, relationships and comparison objects are explicit, results have hierarchy, terminology is stable, and no sentence gains strength beyond its evidence. Record target selection as deferred and reserve journal fit for a later `TARGET_PROFILE_REBASE`.

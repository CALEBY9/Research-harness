# Manuscript Review

Default to **one evidence-grounded review** of the supplied manuscript or passage.
Respect a request for only significant problems. Do not create multiple reviewers,
an editorial synthesis, a concern quota or extra review passes by default.

## Scope and evidence

- Infer the assessment boundary from the request and available manuscript. Partial
  material permits a bounded review, not invented Methods, controls or findings.
- Reuse relevant verified statistics, evidence audits and prior findings when their
  version and scope still apply. Use another owner only for a concrete unresolved
  issue; formal review is not a mandatory audit/statistics/judgment chain.
- Apply the target journal and research design. Local Nature sources explain
  Nature-style criteria, not universal submission policy. Use
  [review axes](references/review-axes.md) or the applicable
  [domain checks](references/domain-specific-review-gates.md) only as needed.
- For readiness or claim-licensing disputes, read
  [evidence authority](../nature-shared/core/evidence-authority.md).
  Artifact presence, model scores and structural checks do not license claims.

## Review

Identify the central claim and its evidence; assess originality, importance,
technical soundness and clarity within the supplied material. Each substantive
concern needs a stable issue ID, a faithful manuscript pointer, supporting evidence, its impact and
an actionable resolution. Missing locations remain unverified. Use the
[technical taxonomy](references/technical-concern-taxonomy.md) for an identified
coverage need, not to manufacture more comments.

Classify concerns by scientific impact. A Major Concern is blocking only when
the central case cannot be established until it is resolved. Local presentation
issues stay Minor unless they affect inference or reproducibility. Preserve
uncertainty, adverse evidence and unknowns; do not invent reviewer identities,
experiments, citations or acceptance probabilities.

## Deliverable

Lead with the assessment and material limitations. Then provide Major Concerns
and Minor Comments with evidence pointers and resolution tests, or only the
requested significant issues. State when a tier has no grounded concern.
Keep the result a referee assessment, not an author response or editorial decision.
Before delivery, verify pointers, severity, unsupported claims and consistency
with the manuscript. Keep internal checklists out of the report.

## Explicit multi-review requests

Only when the user requests multiple reviewer reports, read
[multi-review mode](references/multi-review.md). Separate contexts are needed to
claim mutual blindness. Selecting this skill does not authorize delegation:
without explicit authorization, use permitted separate invocations or disclose
the isolation limit and provide a bounded single-context review. Never label
shared-context reviews independent. Do not add reviewers merely to obtain consensus.

## Writing-depth supplement (2026-09-22)

After the author's approval, the following task-loaded guidance was added to
avoid reducing ARS's writing methods to a short principles-only summary:

| Upstream method | Local carrier | Adaptation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Central thesis, CER, strongest objections and logical flow | `argument-synthesis.md` | Keep the reasoning; omit fixed subargument counts and numerical self-scores |
| Territory, niche, purpose and Introduction failure diagnosis | `introduction-narrative.md` | Keep the moves and matching tests; omit fixed move/paragraph ratios |
| Evidence-integrated drafting and meaning-preserving revision | `revision-diagnostics.md` | Keep source, version and claim checks; no material-passport or patch-applier dependency |
| Context-sensitive writing diagnostics | `revision-diagnostics.md` | Keep precision and structure diagnosis; no vocabulary bans or punctuation quotas |

Additional source read at the same pinned commit:
[Writing Quality Check](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills/blob/a1819d82c36969d9137f7f621916536262af6b4f/academic-paper/references/writing_quality_check.md).
The three references are adapted Chinese guidance, with local cross-study

upstream files, imported agents, verified scientific cases, or copies of the

Full role orchestration and mechanical protocols remain omitted; their useful
scientific functions (source support, uncertainty, author authority, citation
identity and revision meaning) are retained locally. Omission is not a claim
that those upstream protocols have no value in their original full-suite context.
Behavioral equivalence to upstream and preservation of submission-level writing
quality have not been established. Use actual manuscripts, not instruction
length or static checks, to assess that question.

## Targeted local refinement (2026-09-28)

Local failure review sharpened three existing references: distinguish cited
facts from the inference connecting them; motivate descriptive comparisons by
the scientific judgment their answers inform; expand beyond a convenient
review-heavy reading pack when a key premise needs original evidence.
These are local refinements, not newly imported upstream methods. CARS/CER,
conditional guidance, license and author decision boundaries are retained.
The upstream pin and omitted orchestration are unchanged. This update is
not evidence of general writing-quality improvement or upstream equivalence.

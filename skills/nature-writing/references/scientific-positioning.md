# Compare scientific positions

Use to carry a supported scientific position into the manuscript. A standalone
choice among contributions or scientific mainlines belongs to `nature-scientific-insight`,
resolved through the installed skill catalogue; Nature remains the writing owner.
Reuse that skill's current assessment, or its relevant criteria when a consequential
scientific choice remains open. This reference translates the supported question
and evidence into prose; it does not load ARS or repeat an accepted judgment.

## Begin with what is actually measured

Recover the accepted question, definitions, independent unit, population,
denominator, comparison and current result status. Keep historical findings,
current verified findings and proposed analyses separate. An extraction result
is not independent validation. A category containing implemented knowledge
cannot be renamed "mere mention" to create a sharper story. A nested category
defines the ordering of set sizes, not the size or meaning of its conditional
proportion; a higher proportion is not automatically better quality.

Retain the author's approved question groups and outline. Improve how their
answers relate without silently selecting a narrower, easier-to-write study.

## Compare the knowledge gained

Develop distinct candidates only when the material supports a real choice.
Two or three may suffice; no quota or model-generated winner score is needed.
Use the existing argument companion to compare:

- What the closest original studies already allow readers to conclude, and
  what this study adds: a better estimate, discriminated explanation, boundary,
  useful resource or newly comparable evidence. Explain why the added knowledge
  matters to the relevant readers. A dramatic reversal is not required.
- Which scientific question the candidate asks and which present comparison
  can answer it. A different introduction is not necessarily a new scientific
  position; a new causal account may require a different design.
- The strongest alternative explanation and the observation that would
  distinguish it. Specify whether that observation exists, remains uncertain
  or needs further analysis. Proposed explanatory measurements must not merely
  recode the outcome they purport to explain.
- The smallest additional comparison that would change the judgement, if
  current evidence cannot separate the accounts. Suggesting it does not
  authorize new analyses, protocol changes or external model calls.
- The null or contrary finding that would weaken the account, and the narrower
  contribution that could remain. Non-significance does not establish equality.
- Any change to the approved question, population, measurement, causal strength
  or article type. Novelty remains unresolved when the decisive prior sources
  are unavailable; do not manufacture an unoccupied niche.

Recommend with comparative scientific reasons. The author decides a changed
central question or claim. When none is supported, name the missing evidence
and complete the unaffected work instead of polishing an unsupported story.

## Carry the supported question into the artifact

Connect established knowledge, the unresolved distinction and the actual answer
using the Introduction guidance. Each promised contribution must map to a
Results comparison or to a clearly prospective proposal. When figures are
requested, expose that same comparison through the figure plan.

Deliver the requested complete prose; candidate names, evaluation notes and
unresolved scientific choices belong in its separate companion. Do not turn a
positioning exercise into an outline-only stopping point.

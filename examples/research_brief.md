# 从已有观测形成可检验问题 / From observations to a testable question

这是合成教学输入，用来开始一次真实技能调用；以下数字不是实地观测，不支撑任何科研结论。没有私有知识库、账户或论文下载要求。

## 输入材料

某河口有上游、养殖邻近区、外海三个站点，仅在一个夏季各采样四次。养殖邻近区的表层叶绿素 a 较高，同时盐度较低；采样时间、潮位和流量没有同步。没有营养盐通量、稳定同位素、连续水动力或养殖投饵记录。当前作者想判断：这些资料能否支持“养殖排放驱动河口富营养化”这一主张？

## 可直接提交的请求

> 调用 Research agent。仅根据上述合成材料，判断当前资料能支持到哪一步，区分养殖排放、河流输入和水动力聚集等竞争解释，并提出最能改变判断的最小补充观测。保留未知项，不捏造统计结果或文献，不把研究方案写成已完成实验。先完成判断与建议，不撰写论文正文、不下载数据、不安装软件。

## English input and request

Synthetic teaching scenario: an estuary has upstream, aquaculture-adjacent and offshore stations, sampled four times each in one summer. Surface chlorophyll-a is higher and salinity is lower near aquaculture. Sampling time, tide and discharge were not synchronized. Nutrient fluxes, stable isotopes, continuous hydrodynamics and feeding records are unavailable. The proposed claim is that aquaculture discharge drives estuarine eutrophication.

> Use $research-agent. Using only these synthetic facts, assess what the evidence supports, compare aquaculture discharge, river inputs and hydrodynamic aggregation as explanations, and propose the smallest additional observations that could change the judgment. Preserve unknowns. Do not invent statistics or citations, describe plans as completed experiments, write manuscript prose, download data or install software.

## 人工核对 / What to inspect

- 观察、竞争解释、因果主张和拟议验证是否区分清楚？ / Are observations, hypotheses, causal claims and proposed validation distinguished?
- 是否保留采样不同步等限制，并说明哪些新证据会改变判断？ / Are sampling limitations retained and decision-changing observations identified?
- 是否遵守此次范围，没有新增虚构结果、引用或工具动作？ / Does the response preserve scope without invented results, citations or tool actions?

这是一份可复用输入，不是预先写好的标准答案或已通过的模型评测。安装检查通过后，需在自己的宿主实际运行并核对输出。
